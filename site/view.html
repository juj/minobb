Bounding boxes (JSON)
Drop a glTF model here
A .glb file, or a .gltf file together with its .bin and texture files (or the folder that has them).
The page computes the convex hull of the model, its minimum volume oriented bounding box (OBB) and its axis-aligned bounding box (AABB) with minobb. The calculation runs in your browser: the model is not uploaded anywhere.
